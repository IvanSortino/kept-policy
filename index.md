# Kept — Privacy policy

[Italiano](it)

Effective 5 October 2026. Kept is a habit tracker for Android made by Ivan Sortino. Contact:
[kept.habit.tracker@gmail.com](mailto:kept.habit.tracker@gmail.com).

## The short version

Your data stays on your phone and, if you turn sync on, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives any of
it. Kept has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What Kept stores, and where

Everything you enter (habits, check-ins, notes, to-dos, grocery lists, tags and settings) is stored on your phone.

Sync is optional. When you connect Google Drive in Settings, Kept keeps a copy in the hidden app folder of your own
Google Drive. Only Kept can read that folder: it isn't visible in your Drive files, and the developer can't see it.
The copy travels over an encrypted connection.

Android may also back up the app's data to your Google account, as it does for most apps. Google handles that backup.

## Health Connect

If you turn Health Connect on, Kept reads, and never writes, these kinds of data: steps, active calories burned, total
calories burned, nutrition (calories eaten), sleep and exercise sessions (workouts). It reads them to fill the habits
and to-dos you link to them, for example "walk 8,000 steps". With your permission, it also reads them in the background
every 30 minutes, so your habits and widgets stay current.

The values it reads are saved in your habits on your phone and, with sync on, in your Drive copy. Health data is never
used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone. Kept's use of Health Connect data follows the
[Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134),
including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your calendar

If you turn the calendar on, Kept reads the events on your phone's calendars to show them on Today, and writes your
timed to-dos into the calendar you choose. A to-do made from an event keeps that event's id, which travels in your
Drive copy with the to-do. Events are never sent anywhere else.

## Purchases

Kept is free. An optional one-time purchase removes the support pop-up. Google Play handles the payment; Kept only asks
Google Play whether you own the purchase and keeps that answer on your phone.

## Deleting your data

- Uninstalling Kept deletes everything it stored on your phone.
- To delete the Drive copy, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Kept → Delete hidden app data.
- Kept has no accounts of its own: there is nothing else to delete.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
